Meet your doula

Hi, I'm [Your Name]

I'm a birth doula and the founder of In Faith Birth Support. After witnessing how much a calm, caring presence can change a birth story, I felt called to walk alongside mothers who need a little extra emotional and physical support.

My work blends gentle, evidence-based comfort techniques with quiet faith — prayer, Scripture, and encouragement offered in whatever way brings you peace. Whether this is your first baby or your fifth, whether you're feeling joyful or fearful, you are welcome here exactly as you are.

Above all, I believe every birth is sacred and every mother is worthy of tender, unhurried care. It would be my honor to support you.

What guides us

The values we carry into every birth

These are the quiet promises behind everything we do.

Compassion first

You'll never be rushed or judged. We meet you with warmth, patience, and a genuinely listening heart.

Faith as foundation

Prayer and Scripture are always offered gently and with respect — your beliefs and comfort lead the way.

Informed & empowered

We share trusted information so you can make confident choices that are right for you and your family.

Family included

Partners and loved ones matter. We help the whole family feel confident and supported, too.

Calm & presence

A steady, peaceful presence can change everything. We bring calm into the room when you need it most.

Always reachable

Questions and worries don't keep office hours. We're here for you between visits and on call near your due date.
